About Lumora AI

Welcome to Lumora AI Chatbot! Lumora provides reliable support, resources, and guidance to help individuals improve their mental health and wellbeing.

Seeking help for mental health concerns can be difficult. Lumora aims to make the process easier, comfortable, and accessible anytime you need guidance.

Please note: Lumora AI is not a substitute for professional help. In case of a mental health crisis, contact a licensed professional immediately.

Our Mission

At Lumora, we believe education and awareness are key to mental wellness. Our AI chatbot provides accurate, up-to-date information on mental health topics, including conditions, symptoms, and coping strategies. Lumora also offers exercises and assessments to help you evaluate and improve your mental wellbeing.

Our Team

Lumora AI was developed by a passionate student dedicated to breaking mental health stigma and building a supportive community. Our mission is to make mental health support accessible to everyone.
For questions or feedback, contact the developer at lumora@gmail.com.
